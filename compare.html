Show the playing device's own icon for remote playback

The "Playing on" badge in the top bar and the device button in the player bar always drew a speaker. They now draw the playing device's own icon, the same one the device picker already uses, so a phone is drawn as a phone. Nothing else moves: in every pair only those two icons differ, and with the speaker playing the captures are byte-identical.

Before: c78aa9b (main dd2d5e3 plus the phone-playing demo surface). After: f5596a4 (the same plus the change). The captures were taken from the same two commits before a rustfmt-only line wrap in the demo surface. Captured with --demo-shot on Windows 11 x64 (GNU toolchain, built without MilkDrop). macOS and Linux were not captured.
